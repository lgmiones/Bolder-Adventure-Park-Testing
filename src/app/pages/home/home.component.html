The park is split by ground level and aerial activities — our ticket options are simple to understand.

The Jump Pad, Adventure Nets and Exploration Playground are perfect for smaller kids. More daring ones can also ride the Tubing Slides, Bumper Cars and fight it out in the Blaster Zone — all available with a Base Camp Pass.

For bigger thrills, there are two ways to explore Bolder Adventure Mountain: the Ropes Course, including Zip Lines, and the Mountain Course featuring an exhilarating Free Fall Drop!
